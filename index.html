Spending support

Scan a receipt, say what you spent, or type it. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Need help? Email ad.z.yuan@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were trying to do. Never send receipts or personal financial details you don't want to share.

Common questions

Does the app upload my receipts or expenses?

No. Receipt text and speech are recognised on your iPhone, and all data is stored only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Voice capture says it isn't available

Spending only uses on-device speech recognition. If your iPhone doesn't support it for your current language, use Type it instead. Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed in Settings → Spending.

The amount or date on a scanned receipt is wrong

Every capture opens as a draft. Anything the app is unsure about is flagged for you to confirm, and you can edit any field before saving. Tap the receipt photo to view it full screen.

How do I change my currency?

Go to Settings → Default currency. Reports use this currency; expenses in other currencies are listed but not converted or added to report totals.

How do I see a whole year?

Open the Report tab. Use the arrows or swipe to change year, tap a bar to open that month, and use the category menu to filter.

How do I get my data out?

In the Report tab, tap Export CSV to share a year or month as a spreadsheet file.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. There is no copy anywhere else, so this cannot be undone.

Is the tax label tax advice?

No. Labels like "Potentially tax-related" are prompts to help you review your own records. Check with a tax professional about what you can claim.
