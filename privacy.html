Privacy Policy

Spending (iPhone app) · Effective 29 October 2026

In short: the developer does not collect any of your data.
  • Your expenses, receipt photos and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Receipt text and speech are recognised on your iPhone. Voice audio is never saved.
  • There is no account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • The app makes no network requests and contains no third-party SDKs.

Information stored on your iPhone

To work, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

WhatWhy
Expense details: merchant, amount, currency, date, category, purpose, tax review label and noteTo list your expenses and build your reports
Text recognised from a receipt, or the transcript of what you saidTo explain the suggested category and let you check the draft
Receipt photo (only if you choose to keep it)So you can view the receipt later
Your categories and keywordsTo suggest categories
Settings such as default currency and date orderTo remember your preferences

Permissions

Camera

Used only when you scan a receipt. Text is read on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. The photo is kept only if you leave "Keep photo on this iPhone" switched on (the default can be changed in Settings).

Photos

When you choose a receipt photo, iOS gives the app only the photo you pick. The app cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Microphone and speech recognition

Used only while you dictate an expense. Recognition is required to run on your iPhone; if on-device recognition isn't available for your language, the app asks you to type instead and nothing is sent elsewhere. Audio is never recorded to storage.

You can turn any permission off at any time in the iPhone Settings app. The rest of the app keeps working.

Sharing

The app never shares your data by itself. If you use Export CSV, iOS shows the share sheet and you choose where the file goes (for example Mail or Files). After that, the receiving app's own privacy policy applies.

Backups

Like most iPhone apps, the app's data is included in your device backups if you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by the developer.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can:

  • delete a single expense by swiping it in the Expenses list;
  • use Settings → Delete all receipt photos;
  • use Settings → Delete all data to remove all expenses and custom categories;
  • delete the app, which removes everything stored by it on your iPhone.

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, it cannot be recovered after you delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if optional cloud sync is added in a future version), this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before that version is released.
